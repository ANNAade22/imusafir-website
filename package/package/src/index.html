Founder & CEO Story

From Refugee to Founder. From Invisible to Unstoppable.

In 2013, the world told me I was nobody's problem. I was a refugee in Indonesia with no status, no safety net, and no one coming to save me.

Most people would have waited. I started moving.

I didn't have a network. So I built one. I didn't have doors open. So I kicked them down. For 13 years, I traveled across continents, not as a tourist, but as a force. Connecting founders to investors. Immigrants to employers. Strangers to partners. Watching one introduction turn into empires.

I learned the secret the world tries to hide: The right connection doesn't just change your life. It changes the game.

In 2026, I stopped doing it quietly. I launched iMusafir.

iMusafir means the traveler. But this isn't about sightseeing. This is about crossing borders that other people are too afraid to cross. Curated international experiences. Real business networking. Relationships that don't just open doors, they blow them off the hinges.

I built iMusafir because I spent 13 years proving something: Where you start is just geography. Where you go is decided by who you meet.

From refugee to founder. From invisible to unstoppable.

This isn't my story anymore.
This is yours.
