Outdoor Wellness Initiative

Adventure. Wellness. Community.

The iMusafir Outdoor Wellness Initiative is a culturally inclusive hiking and camping program for young women and men ages 18–34.

The pilot will help Minnesota young adults—particularly those from immigrant and underserved communities—experience the physical, emotional, and social benefits of outdoor activity.

What Participants Will Experience

Sports & movement

Active outdoor experiences that get young adults moving with confidence.

Fitness outdoors

Guided activity that builds strength, stamina, and healthy habits.

Training & preparation

Orientation, equipment prep, and practice before bigger adventures.

Guided outdoor activity

Professionally led hikes and movement that build comfort in nature.

Leadership & confidence

Team challenges and coaching that grow voice, courage, and skills.

Wellness & community

Reflection, belonging, and culturally considerate care throughout.

  • Outdoor orientation and equipment preparation
  • Three professionally guided hikes
  • Camping and outdoor-safety training
  • A supervised weekend camping experience
  • Leadership and confidence-building activities
  • Wellness, reflection, and community connection
  • Culturally considerate meals, clothing, and prayer arrangements

Fall 2026 Pilot

The proposed eight-week pilot will serve approximately 25 participants. Transportation, equipment, meals, permits, safety planning, trained outdoor leaders, and participation scholarships will be provided through sponsor support.

Who Can Participate?

The pilot is designed for young women and men who:

  • Are between 18 and 34 years old
  • Live in Minnesota
  • Are interested in hiking, camping, wellness, or leadership
  • May have limited previous access to outdoor activities
  • Can participate in the Fall 2026 program

No previous hiking or camping experience is required.

From Minnesota to the World

After testing and measuring the Minnesota pilot, iMusafir plans to adapt the program for communities across its international network.

During iMusafir’s Indonesia delegation from October 12–24, 2026, we will also explore manufacturers and product concepts for Muslim-friendly activewear, including sports hijabs, breathable long-sleeved tops, and performance clothing offering greater coverage.
