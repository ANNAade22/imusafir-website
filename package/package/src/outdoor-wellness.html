iMUSAFIR BEYOND BORDERS

Outdoor Wellness Initiative

Where Adventure Becomes Belonging

iMusafir Beyond Borders is a culturally inclusive outdoor-wellness initiative bringing Minnesota adults together through hiking, camping, leadership, and community.

Our first eight-week pilot will help 25 participants explore nature, build confidence, strengthen their well-being, and create meaningful connections—regardless of their previous outdoor experience.

Fall 2026 · Minnesota · Ages 18–34

The Outdoors Should Belong to Everyone

For many people, the first step outdoors can feel like the hardest one.

The cost of equipment, limited transportation, lack of experience, and cultural concerns can prevent people from participating. iMusafir Beyond Borders is designed to remove those barriers and create an experience where everyone feels prepared, respected, and welcome.

This is more than a hiking program. It is an opportunity to discover new strength, build leadership skills, improve wellness, and become part of a supportive community.

Your Journey Starts Here

Prepare

Participants begin with orientation, equipment preparation, outdoor-safety education, and an introduction to the program community.

Explore

Three guided Minnesota hikes gradually build strength, confidence, outdoor skills, and connection with nature.

Camp

Participants put their new skills into practice during a supervised weekend camping experience.

Grow

The journey concludes with reflection, leadership development, participant storytelling, and a community celebration.

More Than an Outdoor Adventure

Participants will have opportunities to:

  • Improve physical and mental wellness
  • Build confidence and leadership skills
  • Learn hiking, camping, and outdoor-safety skills
  • Develop healthy and active habits
  • Connect with people from different backgrounds
  • Experience Minnesota’s natural beauty
  • Discover a stronger sense of belonging
  • Become part of a growing global community

Designed with Care

Participant well-being will guide every part of the program.

The final pilot plan will include:

  • Trained outdoor leaders and appropriate supervision
  • Equipment preparation and safety education
  • Weather, route, and emergency planning
  • Permits and insurance confirmed before activities begin
  • Progressive activities suitable for beginners
  • Culturally considerate meals and hydration
  • Prayer accommodations when needed
  • Modest outdoor-clothing options
  • Consideration of individual access needs

Final activities, locations, staffing, and safety procedures will be confirmed before the pilot begins.

Who Can Participate?

The Fall 2026 pilot is intended for people who:

  • Are between 18 and 34 years old
  • Live in Minnesota
  • Are interested in hiking, camping, wellness, or leadership
  • Can participate in the Fall 2026 program
  • Want to meet new people and try something meaningful
  • May have limited previous access to outdoor activities

No previous hiking or camping experience is required.

Joining the interest list does not guarantee placement. Final dates, locations, costs, and participation details will be shared after program funding and planning are confirmed.

Born in Minnesota. Built to Travel.

Beyond Borders begins in Minnesota, but the vision reaches much further.

After evaluating the pilot’s safety, participation, and community impact, iMusafir plans to adapt the program through its international network.

Each future experience will reflect the culture and needs of its community while preserving the same purpose: movement, confidence, leadership, connection, and belonging.

Our vision is to create a global community where people can explore new places, discover their potential, and feel at home wherever the journey takes them.

Wellness Without Barriers

During iMusafir’s Indonesia delegation from October 12–24, 2026, we plan to explore relationships with manufacturers of high-quality modest activewear.

Potential products include sports hijabs, breathable long-sleeved tops, and performance clothing designed for people who prefer greater coverage.

This future initiative supports our larger vision: making fitness, wellness, and outdoor participation more inclusive for everyone.
